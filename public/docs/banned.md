# Account Bans

If your account is banned, you may have violated our Terms, Community Guidelines, or safety rules.

This page explains what a ban means, common reasons, and how to appeal.

## What a Ban Means

- You cannot log in or use account features.
- Your content may be hidden while a review is in progress.
- Repeated or severe violations can lead to permanent removal.

## Common Reasons for a Ban

- Harassment, threats, hate, or targeted abuse.
- Sharing explicit, exploitative, or illegal content.
- Impersonation, fraud, scams, or coordinated misinformation.
- Spam, bot activity, or platform manipulation.
- Evading previous moderation actions.
- Attempting to access, scrape, or disrupt services without authorization.

Please review the [terms of service](/terms) and [community guidelines](/community-guidelines) for more information.

## Ban Types

### Temporary Ban

- Your account is banned for a period of time (3 days, 7 days, 14 days).
- Additional violations after a temporary ban may become permanent.

### Permanent Ban

- Your account is permanently banned & data is deleted.

## Appeals

If you believe your account was falsely banned, you can submit an appeal by contacting us at account.appeals@kang.software.

In your appeal, please include:

- Your username and account email.
- A short explanation of why you believe the action was incorrect.

**Please note that you must use your original account email to appeal, or your ticket will automatically be closed**

Appeals are reviewed manually by a human moderator.

### Outcomes

Your appeal outcome will typically be sent to you within 10-20 business days. Once moderators have made their final decision, you will be emailed notifying you of the outcome, which will either be:

- Appeal denied, ban stays in effect
- Accepted, ban lifted, account restored

Please note that you only have 1 chance to appeal. Do not submit further appeal requests after a decision has been made.

## Ban Evasion

Creating alternate accounts to bypass a ban is a policy violation and may result in IP bans.
