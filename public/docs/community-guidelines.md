# Community Guidelines

Last updated: April 1, 2026

## 1. Our Community

Quacky is a platform for open and respectful communication. These guidelines help us maintain a safe, welcoming, and positive environment for all members.

## 2. Be Respectful

Treat other members the way you'd like to be treated. Disagreements are natural, but keep discussions civil and constructive.

- Do not use slurs, hate speech, or derogatory language targeting individuals or groups
- Avoid personal attacks or harassment
- Respect differing opinions and viewpoints
- Engage in good-faith conversations

## 3. No Harassment or Bullying

We do not tolerate any form of harassment, bullying, or coordinated attacks.

- Do not threaten, intimidate, or encourage violence against any person
- Do not engage in targeted harassment campaigns
- Do not share private information about others without consent
- Do not create content intended to shame or humiliate others

## 4. Respect Intellectual Property

- Do not post copyrighted material without permission
- Give credit to original creators
- Do not plagiarize or claim others' work as your own
- Respect trademarks and brand usage rights

## 5. No Spam or Manipulation

- Do not spam or flood the platform with repetitive content
- Do not artificially inflate engagement through bots or fake accounts
- Do not engage in misleading or deceptive practices
- Do not share malicious links or malware

## 6. Keep Content Appropriate

Quacky is a general-audience social platform and is not intended for children under 13. Be mindful of the content you share.

- Avoid excessive graphic violence or gore
- If sharing sensitive content, use appropriate warnings
- Do not post child sexual abuse material (this is illegal)
- Keep adult content marked appropriately

## 7. Misinformation and Harmful Content

- Do not deliberately spread false information that causes harm
- Do not share medical disinformation that endangers health
- Do not incite violence or illegal activities
- Label satire or opinion clearly

## 8. Self-Harm and Dangerous Behavior

- Do not encourage self-harm or suicide
- If you're struggling, please reach out to a mental health professional
- We provide resources for those in crisis
- Report concerning content immediately

## 9. Account Integrity

- Use your account responsibly and legally
- Do not create fake accounts to evade bans or deceive others
- Do not sell accounts or trade login credentials
- Secure your password and enable two-factor authentication

## 10. Commercial Activity

- No sponsored content is permitted on Quacky
- Do not scam or defraud members

## 11. Political Expression

- Political discussion is allowed if it stays civil and lawful
- Do not harass, intimidate, or target others based on political views
- Do not coordinate manipulation, misinformation, or voter suppression

## 12. Consequences of Violations

- **Warnings:** For minor or first-time violations
- **Temporary suspension:** For repeated or serious violations
- **Permanent ban:** For severe violations or repeated serious breaches

You'll be notified of actions taken and can appeal through our support system.

## 13. Reporting

- Use the report feature for content that violates these guidelines
- Provide context about why you're reporting
- Do not report content simply because you disagree with it
- False reports may result in action against your account

## 14. Exceptions for Privacy and Safety

- We may remove content that reveals private information
- We may act on credible threats to safety
- We may temporarily limit accounts engaged in suspicious activity
- We preserve user data for legal obligations

## 15. Evolving Guidelines

- We'll notify users of significant changes
- We encourage feedback on our policies
- These guidelines apply to all users equally
- Cultural context matters, but core safety principles remain

## 16. Contact & Support

Have questions or need help? Contact support@kang.software for general enquiries. For account appeals, contact us at account.appeals@kang.software.
