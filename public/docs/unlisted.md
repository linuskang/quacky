# Unlisted & Deleted Posts

On Quacky, moderators have the ability to unlist and delete user posts if they violate our [terms of service](/terms) or [community guidelines](/community-guidelines).

When a moderator unlists a post:

- The post is removed from discovery & recommended feeds for all users.
- The post may not appear in public timelines, profile feeds, or search.
- The post can still be view through a direct share link.

### Deleted posts

If moderators find that there is a sufficient violation of our [community guidelines](/community-guidelines), we may choose to delete your post. This means:

- The post is no longer available to view.
- Limited records may still be retained for legal, security, abuse-prevention, and audit purposes.

Your account may also face moderation actions.

## Appeals

If you believe a moderation action on a post was incorrect, you can submit an appeal by contacting us at account.appeals@kang.software

Please include:

- Your username and account email
- A link or identifier for the affected post
- A short explanation of why you believe the decision should be reviewed

**Please note that you must appeal moderation actions on your account using your original user email, or your appeal will be automatically denied.**

### Review Outcomes

Your appeal outcome will typically be sent to you within 10-20 business days. Once moderators have made their final decision, you will be emailed notifying you of the outcome, which will either be:

- Appeal denied, moderation action stays in effect
- Accepted, moderation actions are lifted

Please note that you only have 1 chance to appeal. Do not submit further appeal requests after a decision has been made.
