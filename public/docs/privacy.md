# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Quacky ("we," "our," "us," or the "Service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use Quacky and our services at https://quacky.linus.my. By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this policy where permitted by law.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use Quacky, we collect:

- **Account Information:** Email address, username/handle, name, password (hashed for security)
- **Profile Information:** Bio, profile picture, display preferences, and account settings
- **Content:** Posts, replies, likes, reposts, and any other content you create and share
- **Communications:** Messages, feedback, and support requests you send us

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you access the Service, we automatically collect:

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, browser type, and unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features accessed, posts viewed, time spent on platform, and interaction patterns
- **Technical Information:** IP address, HTTP requests, API calls, and server logs
- **Cookies & Tracking:** Session cookies to maintain your login and analytics cookies to understand platform usage

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party services you connect to Quacky, such as authentication providers.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Quacky uses the collected information to:

- **Provide & Operate Services:** Create and maintain your account, deliver features, and enable social interactions
- **Improve Services:** Analyze usage patterns, understand user behavior, and develop new features
- **Security & Safety:** Monitor for threats, prevent fraud, detect unauthorized access, and enforce our policies
- **Communication:** Send account updates, notifications, and service announcements (you can opt out of non-essential communications)
- **Legal Compliance:** Fulfill legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

Where required by law, we rely on legitimate interests, contract performance, consent, or legal obligation to process personal data.

## 4. Data Storage & Infrastructure

Quacky uses the following service providers to store and process data:

- **Cloud Infrastructure:** Vercel and other third-party providers for hosting
- **Resend:** Email delivery service for account notifications

We may also use analytics, monitoring, and moderation tools to operate, secure, and improve the Service.

Your data may be stored and processed on servers located in different regions depending on your location and the services we use. Quacky takes reasonable precautions to ensure data protection regardless of storage location.

**We do not sell your personal information to third parties.**

## 5. Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, most personal data is removed within 30 days, unless we must retain it for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons
- **Usage Logs:** Automatically collected usage data is retained for 180 days for security and analytics purposes
- **Content:** Your posts and created content are retained until you delete them or your account is terminated, subject to backup and legal retention requirements
- **Backups:** Data in backups may persist for longer periods for disaster recovery purposes

Quacky may retain information longer if required by law or to fulfill other purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy.

## 6. Disclosure of Your Information

We may share your information with:

- **Public Disclosure:** Your profile, handle, bio, and posts are publicly visible by default
- **Legal Obligations:** When required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety
- **User Interactions:** Other users can see your profile and content based on your privacy settings

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access:** Request and review all personal information we have collected about you. You can access this information by going to https://kang.software/takeout
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your account and associated personal data
- **Opt-Out:** Opt out of marketing communications and non-essential data collection

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@kang.software. We will respond within a reasonable time and within any period required by applicable law.

## 8. Security

Quacky implements reasonable technical, administrative, and physical security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

**In the event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify you promptly and take appropriate steps to mitigate impact.** If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, contact us immediately at security@kang.software.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Quacky is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete such information and terminate the child's account immediately.

If you believe your child has signed up for Quacky without proper consent, please contact us immediately at privacy@kang.software and we will promptly delete the account.

## 10. Third-Party Services

Quacky may contain links to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services before providing your information.

## 11. International Data Transfers

User data is primarily stored on secure servers located in Australia and the European Union. While we strive to keep data within your region, transfers across other countries may occur as required by our infrastructure. By using Quacky, you consent to such transfers.
